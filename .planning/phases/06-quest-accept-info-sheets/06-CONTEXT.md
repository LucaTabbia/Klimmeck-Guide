# Phase 6: Quest Accept & Info Sheets - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-10-21
**Status:** Ready for planning (**no UI phase**, user decision 2026-10-21: every visual Claude builds is provisional and will be reworked by the user)

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Users view per-type quest details in the `QuestInfoSheet` above the board and accept a quest with the swipe-left "tear paper" gesture (haptic feedback); on success the app moves to the Map tab where a torn paper for the standard lane stays visible while the quest is active; `story`/`worldMission` quests leave the board as soon as their lane is populated and run in parallel with the standard `pendingQuest`; boss/enemy/aid quests already full show as torn for everyone; acceptance failures roll the optimistic tear back and surface a domain error. The backend contract is BE Phase 7 (`acceptQuest`, lanes, `serverTime`, merged on BE `develop` with PR #10) plus the BE Phase 8 amendments discussed the same day (see D-16).

Amended in this discuss (recorded in REQUIREMENTS.md by the first plan): **QUEST-05** — no quest costs coins, only `story`/`worldMission` cost `requiredPoints` channel points; **QUEST-07** — no live countdown is shown, the torn paper shows a server-driven state label instead; **QUEST-03** — the swipe is the confirmation (no dialog, no undo), the accepted quest's paper stays on the Map; **QUEST-04** — an accepted `story`/`worldMission` stays on the board **torn for its owner** instead of hidden, and the board is reachable only from a city marker.

Out of scope: travel rendering, the travel countdown and the `serverTime` offset (FE Phase 7), disabling the board during travel (FE Phase 7), quest outcomes (completed/failed, auto-generated `aid` quests, death — Phase 9), swipe-accept from the Map overlay, `crime`/`guard` layouts.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Acceptance flow, cost and errors (QUEST-02, QUEST-03, QUEST-05, QUEST-06)
- **D-01:** **The swipe is the confirmation.** No confirmation dialog for any type and **no undo window** (the backend has no cancel for quests or travel). The gesture is a custom `GestureDetector` with a drag threshold (ROADMAP decision, **not** `Dismissible`), swipe-left on the `QuestInfoSheet` **above the board only** — not on the board list and not on the sheet shown inside the Map (`quest_info_overlay.dart` stays read-only).
- **D-02:** **Cost line only for `story`/`worldMission`:** `Punti canale: {twitchPoints} / {requiredPoints}` replaces the hard-coded `3000` in `quest_info_sheet.dart`, highlighted (red/ink emphasis) when the balance is insufficient; `twitchPoints` is the live value from `AuthAuthenticated.user` (`userUpdated`, Phase 3 D-18). **No coins cost for any type** (BE handoff §7): `recommendedLoot` is a display-only hint (D-10). The first plan amends QUEST-05 in `REQUIREMENTS.md`.
- **D-03:** **Optimistic tear, navigation on success:** the tear animation starts with the gesture and the `AcceptQuest` mutation fires when the threshold is crossed; the app switches to the Map tab **only when the backend answers `successful: true`**. On rejection the two halves recompose on the sheet (spring back) and the error surfaces (D-04). The populated lane and the new balance arrive through `characterUpdated`/`userUpdated` — the mutation's `CommonResponse` is never used to build state.
- **D-04:** **Errors in the in-app banner of Phase 5** (`InAppNotificationsCubit.publish` → `KGNotification(title, description)`, parchment strip, auto-dismiss ≈ 5 s, Phase 5 D-17/D-18): Italian domain copy chosen by **`extensions.code`** (BE handoff §2 table: `QUEST_SLOT_BUSY`, `QUEST_FULL`, `QUEST_ALREADY_TAKEN`, `QUEST_TITLE_MISMATCH`, `QUEST_NOT_IN_CITY`, `QUEST_NOT_ACCEPTABLE`, `INSUFFICIENT_POINTS`, `NOT_FOUND`, `FORBIDDEN`; BE Phase 8 adds `TRAVEL_IN_PROGRESS`), never by message text; network/unknown → a generic domain line. The Repository maps `OperationException` into a domain error with the code (`docs/rules/graphql.md`); the UI never sees exceptions. QUEST-06 names the "shop-flow pattern": the researcher documents what the shop does today; the banner is the chosen surface for this phase.
- **D-05:** Mutation document `AcceptQuest($request: AcceptQuestRequest!) { acceptQuest(request: $request) { response successful } }` (naming per `docs/rules/naming.md`) in `lib/graphql/mutations/` (file at planner's choice), Repository → Cubit path (`docs/rules/architecture.md`); `request.id` = `currentCharacter.id`, `questId` = the board quest id.

### Tear gesture and the Map (QUEST-02, QUEST-03)
- **D-06:** **Tear = two halves that separate:** the existing parchment SVG (`CloudinaryAssets.emptySheet`) is clipped along a jagged edge; **no new asset**, all in Flutter. Haptic feedback via `HapticFeedback` (flutter/services): one at the threshold and one at the tear (exact pattern at Claude's discretion). Drag threshold and direction at Claude's discretion (provisional, user-reviewed). **Amended 2026-10-22 (device UAT, user decision):** the left swipe stays; the cut is a horizontal jagged line at 10% of the paper height (e.g. 80 px of 800 px); the top strip stays on the board, the lower piece follows the finger left and, once torn, keeps sliding left until fully off-screen (no clipping at mid screen: the paper's Stack and the sheet scroll view use `Clip.none`); a rejection recomposes it with the spring. The jaggedness is irregular, generated from a `Random` seeded with the quest id (deterministic per quest — never an unseeded Random). Still provisional per D-19.
- **D-07:** **Persistent quest paper on the Map:** after success the app switches to the Map tab and the **intact board asset** of the accepted quest (same per-type image family as `sheet.dart`, **not** a torn one) stays visible for the **standard lane** (placement at Claude's discretion, provisional) for as long as the quest is active; **tapping it opens the same `QuestInfoSheet` presentation read-only with the state line added** (D-15). Centering the map on the quest marker is Claude's discretion (the character will start travelling there automatically — BE Phase 8 D-02 — rendered by FE Phase 7). **Amended 2026-10-25:** while the lane waits for the party the sheet opened from the paper offers "Abbandona il gruppo" with a parchment confirmation (`abandonQuest`); a party not filled within 24 hours is disbanded by the BE (push `questCancelled`, lane emptied via `characterUpdated`).
- **D-08:** **`story` and `worldMission` never show a paper on the Map.** After acceptance they stay on the board **torn for their owner** (existing `storyTornSheet` assets; QUEST-04 amended: torn, not hidden) and open read-only; no other "waiting for the GM" indicator in this phase.

### Info sheet layouts (QUEST-01)
- **D-09:** **Three layout families**, variants only where the content differs: **combat** (`hunt`, `enemy`, `boss`, `dungeon`): enemy image + name (+ grade when the model has it), required time, party for `boss`; **narrative** (`story`, `worldMission`): the text of the linked lore (`infos.relatedLore`) **readable inside the sheet, scrollable**, before cost (D-02) and party (`worldMission`); **service** (`heal`, `aid`, `job`, `study`): required time, rewards, recommended loot. `crime`/`guard` out of scope. The rewards block stays as today.
- **D-10:** **Eligibility/state indicators on the sheet** (all four): **required title** (`requirements.minTitle`, highlighted when the character is not eligible — exact title for `story`, minimum for the others, `TitleType` order rookie < adventurer < paladin < mage < hero < legend); **slots per rank** (`requirements.party` `{ title, count }` with the registered count per rank from `registeredAdventurers { character title }`, D-14) for `worldMission`/`boss`/`enemy`/`aid`; **already done** for non-repeatable types (`story`, `worldMission`, `enemy`, `boss`, `aid`) present in `completedQuests` or with the character already registered; **recommended loot** (`requirements.recommendedLoot`) where present.

### Board, lanes and the torn state (QUEST-04)
- **D-11:** **Board access and content.** The board is reachable **only when the character stands on a city marker** (`status.location` equals a `City.markerLocation`; from a quest POI the satchel item is disabled and its cell darkened — the same treatment the travel case gets in FE Phase 7 with `activeTravel`). Content: all quests of that city (`infos.markerLocation` ∈ the city's `pointsOfInterest`), **its `worldMission`s included, plus only the `story` whose `minTitle` equals the character's title, from any city** (the other four chapters are not listed; BE Phase 8 D-26 lifts the city check for `story` only — a `worldMission` accepted outside its city is answered `QUEST_NOT_IN_CITY`, and the FE classifier marks it `notInCity`) (amended 2026-10-22 by the user: worldMission follows the city rule like the standard lane; only story is exempt — mirrors BE D-26 as amended). Non-repeatable quests already in `completedQuests` are hidden. An accepted `story`/`worldMission` stays listed **torn for its owner** (D-08, D-12). Everything else stays visible: when the client can tell the quest is not acceptable (title, lane busy, full for the rank, torn) the sheet shows why and the swipe is blocked (the paper resists and springs back); **the backend remains the judge** (banner on rejection). A standard quest the player accepted is never met on the board before its outcome: the character leaves the city automatically (BE Phase 8 D-02) and comes back only when the quest is over.
- **D-12:** **"Torn for everyone" on the board, built on what `sheet.dart` already does:** the current asset mapping stays (boss/enemy → hunt sheets, dungeon/aid → quest sheets, hunt/job/guard → job, heal/study → announcement, story → story, worldMission → story; torn variants `huntTornSheet1/2`, `questSheetTorn1/2`, `storyTornSheet1/2`) and the existing `isFull` rule is **extended to rank**: a registered quest (`worldMission`, `boss`, `enemy`, `aid`) is full when the registered count reaches the **sum of the `count`s** of `requirements.requiredAdventurers` — now a **list of slots `{ title?, count }`** (BE Phase 8 D-28: `title` null = any rank, `party` removed) — **or** when the slots of the player's own rank (slots with that `title` plus the `null` ones) are exhausted, counting `registeredAdventurers { character title }`. The owner's own active `story`/`worldMission` is torn too (D-08). A torn quest cannot be swiped. `hunt`, `dungeon`, `job`, `heal`, `study` are never registered, so never torn. Needs BE Phase 8 (D-27/D-28): see D-16.
- **D-13:** **The travel case of the disabled board** (`activeTravel` present) is **deferred to FE Phase 7**; this phase implements the disabled state for "not on a city marker" (D-11) with the visual the travel case will reuse (satchel item disabled, darker cell).
- **D-14:** **Fragment and documents:** `CharacterFragment.questsFields` gains `pendingQuest`, `activeStoryQuest`, `activeWorldMissionQuest` each selecting `{ id startDate waitingTime pointsSpent quest { id } }` (fragment-only change, Phase 3 D-05; `completedQuests { id }` unchanged; `startDate` is parsed nullable already and **is null while travelling**, BE Phase 8 D-15). The quests query adds what the sheet/board need: `requirements { minTitle requiredPoints requiredAdventurers { title count } recommendedLoot { … } }`, `registeredAdventurers { character title }`, `infos { relatedLore { … } enemy { … } }` (no `party`: BE Phase 8 removes it). `Quest.fromJson` is fixed for `registeredAdventurers` (today it parses a `List<Character>` that never matched the wire) and `QuestRequirements.requiredAdventurers` becomes a list of slots (`title` nullable, `count`), with `isFull` in `sheet.dart` rewritten on top of it. The "on a city marker" check of D-11 comes from the cities query (`city_queries.dart`, `markerLocation { id }`) or the POI `type` (planner's choice).

### State label on the torn paper (QUEST-07 amended)
- **D-15:** **No countdown is shown.** The quest paper of the standard lane (D-07) carries a **server-driven state label**, shown in the opened sheet (and, at Claude's discretion, compactly on the paper itself): `startDate == null` → **"In viaggio"** (the character is travelling to the marker, BE Phase 8 D-15; the travel countdown arrives with FE Phase 7); `startDate` set → a **per-type label** — `enemy`/`boss` "In combattimento…", `hunt` "Cacciando…", `dungeon` "Esplorando…", `job` "Lavorando…", `heal` "Guarendo…", `aid` "Aiutando…", `study` "Studiando…" (copy at Claude's discretion, provisional) — kept until the outcome (Phase 9) empties the lane. QUEST-07 is amended accordingly by the first plan; **no `serverTime` query and no clock offset in this phase** (FE Phase 7). **Amended 2026-10-25 (quick 261025-grp, BE Phase 9 plan 09-19):** `startDate == null && arrivedAt == null` → "In viaggio"; `startDate == null && arrivedAt != null` → "In attesa del gruppo k/N" (k registered, N total seats): a registered quest starts only when the party is complete and on the marker (user decision 2026-10-25: "per poterle cominciare il party dev'essere al completo, quindi tutti restano in attesa").

## Specific Ideas

- "Si mostrano tutte le quest accettabili nella città dove ci si trova. La board non è accessibile durante il viaggio (si disabilita il pulsante sul menù e si rende la casella più scura per segnalare che è disabilitata). Mostri quindi tutte le quest relative al posto (la categoria dei job possono essere ripetuti e presi da più persone in contemporanea, anche hunt e dungeon, anche se contengono un combattimento), mentre le missioni story e worldMission sono accettabili in tutte le città. Infine le missioni con una caccia (boss, enemy, aid) una volta accettate da qualcuno, sono visibili come strappate sulla board finché sono ancora pending. Se la quest viene completata con successo allora scompaiono dalla board, se invece falliscono possono essere riprese da qualcun altro (eccetto aid, che scompare) e si vede quindi di nuovo l'asset intero."
- "Quando il numero di registeredAdventurers raggiunge lo stesso numero di requiredAdventurers allora la quest risulta strappata. Se la quest accetta più di un avventuriero, se il tuo rango è completo allora vedrai la scheda strappata, altrimenti intera."
- "Quando si arriva a 0 di viaggio allora compare la dicitura (In combattimento… lavorando… guarendo… esplorando… cacciando…) più azzeccata per il tipo di quest. Il foglio di worldMission e di story non compare mai sulla mappa. La dicitura resta attiva finché non arriva il risultato del combattimento." / "Il countdown del waitingTime non si vede."
- "Asset intero della board. Quando lo clicchi ha la stessa presentazione della missione nella board, ma si aggiunge anche lo stato." (paper on the Map) / "Solo la story del mio titolo." / "[Una quest standard accettata sulla board] è possibile solo con le missioni di tipo story e worldMission, perché negli altri casi saresti o in viaggio o in un altro POI dopo aver accettato la quest e potresti tornare in città solo a quest finita […]. Nel caso di worldMission e story le vedi strappate." / board only in a city: "Sì, solo in città".
- Swipe = confirmation; cost line "saldo / costo" highlighted; tear starts immediately, navigation on success; errors in the Phase 5 banner; two halves that separate; torn paper persistent on the Map, tappable; three layout families; lore inside the sheet.
