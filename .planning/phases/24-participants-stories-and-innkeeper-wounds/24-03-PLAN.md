<objective>
The shared pieces of the two story pages: the promoted background renderer, the per-participant story dropdown and the participants' read that can run before the draft exists.

Purpose: D-03 and D-08 want the same dropdown on the first and on the last page; D-02 wants the stories readable before the mission starts, so the read cannot wait for the draft any more.
Output: `BackgroundEntries` in shared, `StoryReader` + `ParticipantStory`, `ensureLoaded`, `backgroundOf` / `titleOf`.
