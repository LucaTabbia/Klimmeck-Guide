<objective>
The player's side of the innkeeper wounds (ruling 4, D-14, D-15): the Journal marks them, the cure lines leave them out, and the Board rereads when an origin flips.

Purpose: the player recognises a wound no cure will take and never reads a promise the BE will not keep. Against today's BE `innkeeperInjuries` is empty and nothing changes on screen.
Output: Journal line, `curableInjuriesOf`, potion line with «beyond cure», Board reread key.

Technical defaults adopted (declared): the mark in the Journal is the user's line under the wound's name, drawn with a style already in `lib/theme/` (no new asset, research Q9); `InjuryNameIcon` gets an optional `note` (a line under the name) instead of a boolean; `characterInjuriesOf` is deleted if it has no caller left (Boy Scout); `healedInjuriesLine` gains the named parameter `beyondCure` (default empty) and `ItemRow` / `PotionSheet` an optional `injuriesBeyondCure` passed only by the Journal; the shop line stays as it is (to look at in the UAT, ruling 4).
</objective>
