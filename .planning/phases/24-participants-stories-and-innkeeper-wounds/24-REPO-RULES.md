**Naming trap:** the wire step `stories` is the LAST page (the additions); the FE page enum is `MissionPage { stories, place, encounters, fight, prizes, recap, additions }` where `MissionPage.stories` is the FIRST page (the reading page, never saved as a step). `_pageOf(MissionDraftStep.stories)` returns `MissionPage.additions`: say it in the code comment.

**Additions on the save (BE research §Q4):** each entry replaces the one of its character, the others stay; a text empty after trim REMOVES the entry. The app therefore sends ONE entry per participant of the page with the trimmed text, the empty ones included, built in ONE function (`backgroundAdditionsInputOf`). If the BE handoff says otherwise, 24-10 changes that one function.

## Hard rules

- **Gated selection:** every new field is modelled leniently pre-gate and selected ONLY in 24-10. Selecting one earlier makes `character`, `characterUpdated` or the draft fail on today's BE.
- **One switch for the new INPUT:** `lib/config/story_and_wound_corrections.dart` → `const bool storyAndWoundCorrectionsEnabled = false;` (shape of `lib/config/companion_care.dart`), read through a widget parameter that defaults to the constant so tests run both states. Off (pre-gate): the recap keeps today's «Completa» with its confirmation, the additions page is unreachable, a carried wound cannot be removed in the chips — nothing new is ever sent to today's BE. On (flipped by 24-10 only): the recap shows «Avanti», the last page and «Concludi» are live, carried wounds are removable. No other pre-gate plan exposes the new input. The reading page needs nothing new from the BE and is live from 24-04.
- **Rule 4 (backend is the truth):** the app never derives the origin of a wound (no «corrected minus simulated»), never decides what a cure heals, never validates the 1000 characters with authority (counter and disabled «Concludi» are UX). It marks only what is served: `innkeeperInjuriesBefore` and the served `innkeeperInjuries` of the effective effect.
- **Corrections (ruling 1, D-13, D-16):** the only correction surface is the wizard (`InjuryChips`, `correction_section.dart`): the plain pending-request form was removed by plan 21-17 and is NOT rebuilt. Two groups under «Ferite»: «Ferite presenti» (one chip per wound of `injuriesBefore`, selected = kept, tap = removed, an innkeeper wound labelled «<ferita> (dell'oste)») and «Ferite da aggiungere» (every `InjuryType`; a carried wound not removed is disabled; a removed one is addable again = it becomes an innkeeper wound). Un-removing a wound drops it from the added list. Key prefixes: `completion.injury.kept.<name>` and `completion.injury.added.<name>` (the old `completion.injury.<name>` disappears). Served lists are never «repaired» by the app: a seeded correction passes through as served.
- **Reading page (ruling 2, D-01..D-03, D-02a):** the flow opens on it; one closed `Dropdown` per participant («<nome> · <rango>», the rank when known); entries in the served order; «Di questo avventuriero non si sa ancora nulla» for an empty story; the start note and «Inizia la missione» at the bottom; opening never creates the draft; after the start the page is reachable by «Indietro» and shows «Avanti» (local, no save). **No popup anywhere.** A failed read shows the failure line and «Riprova» and never disables «Inizia la missione».
- **Last page (ruling 3, D-04..D-08):** after the recap; per participant the story dropdown and ONE optional field (`SheetTextField`, `maxLength: 1000`, `minLines: 3`, `maxLines: 5`); too long = more than 1000 grapheme clusters after trim (`text.trim().characters.length`, counted in the widget layer, never in `lib/models`) → error text «Massimo 1000 caratteri» and «Concludi» disabled. ONE «Concludi» closes the mission after the confirmation dialog (the recap lines plus «Storia di <nome>: una nuova pagina» per non-empty addition). The texts are owned by the screen State, seeded ONCE from the served draft at the first entry of the page; a new served draft never reseeds them (do not copy `FightStep.didUpdateWidget`). Saved on «Concludi» (save, then completion, one Cubit action), on «Indietro» from the last page and on leaving the wizard from the last page with unsaved text. `SingleChildScrollView` + `Column`, never `ListView.builder` around the dropdowns and the fields.
- **Player side (ruling 4, D-14, D-15):** Journal «Ferite»: an innkeeper wound carries the line «Troppo grave per essere curata» under its name (the line is the mark; no new asset, no app chrome). «Cura le tue ferite: …» (heal quests and every prize with a cure) reads the character's wounds minus the innkeeper's. The potion line «Cura: …» leaves the character's innkeeper wounds out in the Journal item rows and in the potion sheet; the shop, the wizard's prize catalogue and the result sheet keep the catalogue line (shop: to look at in the UAT). `QuestScopeListener` keys the Board reread on `innkeeperInjuries` too.
- **Result sheet (ruling 5, D-09, D-18):** «La tua storia ha una nuova pagina» only when `backgroundEntryAdded` is true; ONE line «Guarite: …» listing `injuriesHealed` followed by the wounds of `injuriesRemoved` not already listed. Never inferred.
- **Sigil (ruling 6):** no new code: tests prove that an entry appended to a background already seen lights the Profile sigil and marks the entry. If such a test is RED, fix it minimally in TDD inside the `NewEntries*` files and report it in the SUMMARY.
- **Wizard tests are pinned on today's shape:** «Passo k di 5», `MissionDraftAbsent` → `StartStep`, «Indietro» disabled on «Luogo», «Completa» on the recap, chips «(D-14)»: those cases are REWRITTEN RED-first, not only added to.
