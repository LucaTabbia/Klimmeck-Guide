<objective>
Wire the last page into the wizard, behind the switch (D-04..D-08): recap → «Avanti» → additions → «Concludi».

Purpose: the innkeeper adds one entry to each participant's story at the very end, rereading that story; leaving and coming back finds the texts. Against today's BE (switch off) the flow ends at the recap exactly as today.
Output: screen and recap rewired, the wizard and recap tests rewritten RED-first for both switch states.

Technical defaults adopted (declared): the screen gets `isStoryAndWoundInputEnabled` (default: the constant) and passes it on; `RecapStep` gains an optional `onNext` — when given, the button is «Avanti» and the page neither confirms nor completes (no boolean flag: the presence of the callback is the mode); the confirmation becomes the top-level `confirmMissionCompletion(context, lines)` used by the recap (switch off) and by the screen for «Concludi» (switch on). If the save made while leaving is refused, the wizard stays and shows the failure line; the NEXT attempt to leave closes without saving (the innkeeper is never trapped offline; the texts already saved stay in the draft) — the flag resets when a text changes.
</objective>
