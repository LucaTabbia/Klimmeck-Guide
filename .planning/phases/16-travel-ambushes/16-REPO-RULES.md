## Product rules that bind every plan

- No app-style chrome on gameplay screens (map, sheets, board, journal): no AppBar, no bottom nav, no loading scaffolding, no spinner; loading only on explicit user action or cold start (rule 2 of CLAUDE.md).
- The backend is the source of truth: the FE never computes probability, enemies, nearest city, rewards or HP; the reroute is rendered from the new `activeTravel` as received (D-03); the pawn interpolates from `startTime` + `duration` as today — no second mechanism.
- Carousel (D-05, D-11..D-13): the consecutive `kind == ambush` results at the head of the FIFO queue open together, one parchment per ambush, chronological, «k di N» as the page indicator; «Continua» advances to the next page and, on the last page, closes the carousel and acknowledges every report of the group (best effort per id); a live ambush triggers a refetch and the presenter waits while it is in flight (`isSettlingAmbushes`); quest results stay one at a time after the carousel; presentation rules of Phase 9 unchanged (free Map beneath routes, `TravelCubit` idle; death anywhere); the `ambushDefeat` toggle never gates the carousel nor the reroute.
- Ambush sheet (D-04): the result sheet with the header «Imboscata presso <nearCity.name>» + date instead of quest title and type; enemies block, HP bar, «Ferite»; won: «Ricompense» (coins) and the XP/level/title lines; lost: the line «Portato a <reachedCity.name>» and no rewards. No «Missione conclusa» banner for ambush results (D-06).
- Defeat notice (D-02): new category end to end (enum, settings toggle «Imboscate», popup → Map, in-app banner from the push `title`/`body`); nothing on a victory. The confirmation sheet and the quest «Viaggio» line stay exactly as Phase 15 left them (D-07), pinned by tests.
