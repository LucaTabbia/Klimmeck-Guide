<objective>
HEAL-06 (CONTEXT D-20, user rule added during the plan): a heal that would change nothing for the current character is shown on the Board as an already torn sheet that cannot be torn or accepted. The backend owns the rule and serves the per-viewer flag `Quest.noEffect`; the FE reads it (lenient → `false`), draws the torn state from the existing tear visuals, rereads the list silently when the character's HP or injuries change, and maps the refusal `QUEST_NO_EFFECT` to an in-world notice with a list reread.

This plan is NOT gated: it runs on fixtures. The selection of `noEffect` in the shared quest fragment is part of the gated plan 18-12; until then the flag is always `false` on a real build.

Purpose: a healthy character does not waste hours in a temple that can do nothing; the sheet says so in the world and comes back whole when the heal is useful.
The refusal `QUEST_NO_EFFECT` of a stale acceptance (failure enum, notice, list reread) is the next plan, 18-11.

Output: 6 lib files modified, 6 test files and 1 fixture modified.
</objective>
