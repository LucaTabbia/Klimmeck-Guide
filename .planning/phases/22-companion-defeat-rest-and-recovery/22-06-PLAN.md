<objective>
Bring the companion state into the Journal «Compagno» section: live life, rest and defeat on the parchments and the slot, the name as title, the companion's potion wait, and the long-press details hosted in the Journal Stack like spells.

Purpose: D-09 is the main thing the player sees; D-22 gives the gesture; the defeated parchment must refuse the tap (D-02) and come back by itself at `fullLifeAt` (D-24).
Output: rows, slot, content and section reading `CompanionVitalityCubit`; `Journal._selectCompanion` + `_buildCompanionSheet`; titled tests.

Technical defaults adopted (declared): the Journal row text column reads, in order, «Al tuo fianco», «Esperienza: x / y», «Vita: x / max» (UNDER the experience line, D-09), the status line (rest/defeat), «Pozione: pronta fra …»; the speed stays on the card (orchestrator ruling: a mount's speed matters for travel), attack and defense live in the details sheet (22-05); the slot life is a `Text` under the slot box keyed `CompanionContent.slotLifeKey`; the rename action of the details sheet is NOT wired here (22-12, behind the switch).
</objective>
