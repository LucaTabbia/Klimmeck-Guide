<objective>
The «account deleted» landing end to end, against today's BE: the new revocation reason read from a refused refresh (HTTP refresh and WS 4409 alike), the local teardown with that reason, the notice of D-03 and the consent memory forgotten only on that path.

Purpose: plan 25-03 ends the own deletion with `terminateSession(accountDeleted)`; the player deleted by the innkeeper while online gets the same landing from the BE's refusal (D-15, D-18). Both converge here.
Output: `UnauthenticatedReason.accountDeleted`, `sessionRevokedReasonAccountDeleted`, `SignInNotice.accountDeleted`, `LegalConsentCubit.forget()`, the AuthGate rows.

Fallback stated (D-15, D-18): after the BE's 15-minute window, or after a BE restart inside it, the refresh answers `SESSION_EXPIRED` and the player reads the session-expired notice; the consent memory is then kept (harmless: the next login sends the version again and the BE records it). Accepted by CONTEXT D-18; no client-side workaround.
</objective>
