<objective>
The research outcome reaches the player (D-29, D-31): the two new result sheets read well, the two new pushes are handled, and the in-session banner says the same thing as the push.

Purpose: «Manderei inoltre una notifica per avvisare l'utente se la sessione di ricerca è andata o meno a buon fine e se è andata a buon fine, quanto ci vuole per imparare la magia» (D-29); «Io un foglio di esito per indicare la magia ottenuta lo metterei però» (D-31).
Output: three result lines, the learning-end sheet without the life bar, the two wire types on `questEnd`, the banner copy by outcome.

How pushes work today (read on 2026-11-16): FCM data-only → `PushEvent.tryParse` → `NotificationCategory.fromWireType(data['type'])`; an unknown type returns null and the message is dropped with «Push dropped: unknown or malformed message» (no notification, no crash). A known personal type becomes a local notification (background) or an in-app banner (foreground) if the category toggle is on; the tap carries `NotificationIntent{category, id}` to the Map and runs the category popup (`questEnd` → `QuestResultsCubit.requestFromTap(id)`).

Design choices (planner, 2026-11-16; vetoable by the orchestrator before execution):
1. **No new category.** After D-31 both research pushes carry a QuestResult id and must open the sheet: they are accepted wire types of `questEnd`. Consequences: governed by the existing «Fine quest» toggle; no new storage key, settings row or popup entry; the push banner and the banner published by `questResultReceived` share the key `questEnd|<id>` and never show twice. Alternative (rejected): two categories with their own toggles — needs the presenter, the settings and the dedup reworked for no asked behaviour.
2. **`legacyWireTypes` is renamed `alsoAcceptedWireTypes`**: it now holds current types too (`ambushDefeat` stays in it).
3. **The result sheet stays text** (Phase 22 rule; `spellLearned { id name }` is text today): «Magia scoperta» is a line, not a spell card.
4. **Banner durations through `formatGameDuration`** («30 min») while the BE push says «30 minuti»: whichever arrives first is shown (dedup); accepted, noted for the UAT copy review.
</objective>
